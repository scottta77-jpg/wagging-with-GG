Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before booking your dog’s groom with Georgia at Waggin with GG.

Get in touch
1

What grooming services do you offer for different breeds?

At Waggin with GG, we offer full grooms, baths and blow-dries, nail trimming, teeth brushing and coat maintenance tailored to all breeds and coat types — from curly Cockapoos and Bichons to double-coated and short-haired dogs.

2

Do you use hypoallergenic and dog-safe products?

Yes. We only use gentle, dog-safe and hypoallergenic shampoos, conditioners and ear cleaners. If your dog has sensitive skin or a vet-prescribed shampoo, just let Georgia know before the appointment.

3

How do you handle nervous or anxious dogs?

Before every groom, Georgia asks what your dog is usually like around brushes, clippers or the dryer. Appointments at Waggin with GG are calm, patient and muzzle-free, giving nervous dogs plenty of reassurance.

4

What safety measures do you take to ensure my dog’s welfare?

Your dog’s wellbeing always comes first at Waggin with GG. We operate a clean, secure, muzzle-free salon in Enfield, check your dog’s coat and skin carefully, and always work at a pace your dog is comfortable with.
